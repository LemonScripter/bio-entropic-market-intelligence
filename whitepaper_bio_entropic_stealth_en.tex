    Manages ACID-compliant SQLite storage (\texttt{data/market\_data.db}), automated CSV/JSON exports, and persistent authenticated Playwright session state injection (\texttt{storage\_state}).

\begin{equation}
\lambda(t) = \lambda_{\text{base}} \cdot \Phi_{\text{circadian}}(t) \cdot \left[1 + \gamma \cdot \frac{A(t) - \bar{A}}{\sigma_A}\right]
\end{equation}

\noindent where the 24-hour diurnal circadian baseline $\Phi_{\text{circadian}}(t)$ is formulated as:

\begin{equation}
\Phi_{\text{circadian}}(t) = \frac{1}{2} \left[1 + \sin\left(\frac{2\pi (t \pmod{24})}{24} - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\right]
\end{equation}

\noindent The resulting operational dwell time $\Delta t$ combines inhomogeneous Poisson realization with biological log-normal micro-jitter:

\begin{equation}
\Delta t = \tau_{\text{Poisson}} + \tau_{\text{jitter}}, \quad \text{where} \quad \tau_{\text{Poisson}} \sim \text{Exp}(\lambda(t)), \quad \tau_{\text{jitter}} \sim \text{Lognormal}(\mu_{\text{jitter}}, \sigma_{\text{jitter}})
\end{equation}

Our stealth layer introduces direct countermeasures:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Native Wheel-Delta Stream Generation:} Synthetic scrolling via JavaScript \texttt{window.scrollTo()} leaves empty \texttt{WheelEvent} buffers. Our engine executes stepped physical wheel dispatches (\texttt{page.mouse.wheel(0, delta)}) in $4-8$ randomized sub-steps with non-linear deceleration.
    \item \textbf{Physiological Click Duration Variance:} Traditional bots dispatch instantaneous click events ($T_{\text{down}} \approx 0\text{ ms}$). We decouple \texttt{mouse.down()} and \texttt{mouse.up()} with a log-normal holding time:
    \begin{equation}
    T_{\text{down}} \sim \text{Lognormal}(\mu = 65\text{ ms}, \sigma = 0.25)
    \end{equation}
    ensuring non-zero $\text{std}(T_{\text{down}})$ that matches human electromyographic fingertip response.
    \item \textbf{Continuous Bézier Pointer Movement:} Mouse paths follow Fitts's Law with minimum-jerk velocity and $8-12\text{ Hz}$ physiological tremor.
\end{enumerate}
